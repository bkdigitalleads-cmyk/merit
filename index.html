Frequently asked questions

How do I track volunteer hours for National Honor Society?
Pick NHS as your goal, enter your chapter's target, and log each shift with the organization and supervisor. The progress bar shows exactly where you stand, and the PDF gives your adviser a dated, signable record.

How do I prove community service hours to my school?
Export the PDF: every entry dated, supervisor columns, totals against your goal, and a certification block. It has everything you need to fill out any school's own form in a minute.

Does it work for court-ordered or program hours?
Yes — the goal picker covers program requirements, and the signed-log format works anywhere a verified hours record is required.
