## Description (EULA + Privacy links REQUIRED — do not remove)

Merit is the volunteer hours tracker that turns every shift into progress you can see — and proof you can hand in.

Whether you're logging community service hours for National Honor Society, college applications, a scholarship, a school graduation requirement, or a court or program requirement, Merit keeps the record clean: date, hours, organization, and the supervisor who can verify it.

LOG HOURS IN SECONDS
• Tap +, enter your hours (2.5 works fine) — or run the shift timer while you volunteer
• Organization autofill remembers where you serve
• Categories for community, school, faith-based, health, animals, and environment work

WATCH YOUR GOAL FILL UP
• Pick your goal — NHS, college apps, scholarship, school, program, or personal
• Set the exact target hours your school or chapter requires
• The progress bar tells you exactly how close you are

HAND IN PROOF, NOT PROMISES
• One tap creates an official-style PDF: dated entries, organization and supervisor columns, totals against your goal, and a certification block with a supervisor signature line
• CSV export for spreadsheets and school portals
• Everything you need to fill out any school's own form in a minute

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Your log lives on your iPhone — no account, no cloud, no tracking
• Optional Face ID lock
• Works fully offline

Free to start: log your first 10 entries on us. Merit Pro unlocks unlimited entries, PDF and CSV export, and Face ID lock — as a one-time Lifetime purchase or a Yearly subscription.
